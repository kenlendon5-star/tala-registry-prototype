## Scoping invariants

- Household sections (1–4, 10–18) carry `scopedToMember = false`; member sections (5–9) carry `scopedToMember = true`. [DisasterProtectionScopingTest](../../../android-native/app/src/test/java/ph/tala/registry/domain/form/DisasterProtectionScopingTest.kt) and [HouseholdServicesScopingTest](../../../android-native/app/src/test/java/ph/tala/registry/domain/form/HouseholdServicesScopingTest.kt) verify that household sections refuse member scopes and keep one shared draft bucket; the member-isolation suites verify the converse.
- All 18 sections save as full-snapshot writes in one Room transaction reporting `Saved` or `Failed` explicitly; device persistence tests verify a real close/reopen and that `member_answers` stays empty for household sections.

## Unresolved choices kept visibly marked for the demo

- **Evacuation areas (section 13)**: `Barangay Hall (Sample)`, `School Evacuation Center (Sample)`, `Covered Court (Sample)` plus `Other` free text, with the field hint "Sample options; confirm the designated local evacuation areas." The labels are asserted in `DisasterProtectionValidationTest` so the sample marking cannot silently disappear.
- **Social protection programs (section 15)**: the option list records what the prototype offered; it is not presented as an authoritative inventory of government programs, and ambiguous prototype source notes were left unresolved rather than guessed (see the revision notes).
- These are recording-inventory decisions for the capstone demo, not defects; replacing the sample evacuation sites requires the barangay's approved list.
