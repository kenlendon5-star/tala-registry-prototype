# D11 — Complete household services

Implemented and verified 16 October 2026 on OPPO CPH2529 (serial e7d5dc18), Android 15 / API 35, app 0.5.0 (versionCode 5), isolated evidence package `ph.tala.registry.d08test`. Day 11 household-level service sections (Health, Food security, Transport & finance, Internet & public safety, Water & sanitation, Housing) with representative validation, branching, multi-select and scoping tests.

## Delivered

- **Section specifications**: all six sections exist natively as generated definitions in `QuestionnaireSections.kt` (regenerated from `planning/docs/schemas/questionnaire-current.json` via `tools/port-native-questionnaire.cjs`, `--check` passes: 18 sections / 94 fields). All six carry `scopedToMember = false` — household fields cannot become member-scoped; `FormSessionViewModel` refuses a member scope for them (`Section and answer scope do not match`).
- **Branching and multi-select behavior** (verified in `HouseholdServicesBranchingTest`):
  - Health: `illness = Yes` reveals treatment; flipping to `No` clears it to a fixed point (`FormRules.update`); unconditional `healthAccess` survives the flip.
  - Internet: `internet = Yes` reveals connection; flipping to `No` clears connection; unconditional `safety` survives.
  - Transport & finance: `accounts` multi-select with exclusive `None` / `Prefer not to answer` via `FormRules.toggle` — an exclusive choice drops normal options, normal options drop exclusive ones, and the two exclusive choices replace each other.
  - Water: `waste` multi-select accumulates and un-toggles without exclusives, per spec.
- **Validation** (verified in `HouseholdServicesValidationTest`): required-field errors for every field, off-list option rejection, hidden-field validation suppression (a hidden `treatment`/`connection` never errors), multi-select membership/duplicate/exclusivity errors, and Housing numeric limits (`rooms` whole numbers 0–50; 2.5, "many", -1 and 51 rejected).
- **Household scoping** (verified in `HouseholdServicesScopingTest`): household answers live in exactly one draft bucket keyed by `AnswerScope.Household`, never per member; member-scoped education answers coexist without leaking; branch clearing applies to the shared bucket.
- **Device persistence** (`HouseholdServicesPersistenceTest`, androidTest): saves all six sections to `household_answers` (scalars and multi JSON), reopens a real Room file, verifies every row round-trips through `AnswerCodec`, verifies `member_answers` stays empty, and verifies a re-save replaces exactly the rows in the new full-snapshot write.
- **Device harness**: `tools/verify-d11-device.ps1` runs the persistence test plus education/demographics/storage and UI regressions on the isolated `.d08test` build, preserving evidence in `planning/03-evidence/day11/`.
