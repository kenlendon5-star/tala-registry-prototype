## Delivered

- **Education section (member-scoped)**:
  - Attendance branching: `attending = Yes` reveals school level and strand/track; `No` reveals the non-attendance reason instead, with hidden dependent answers cleared to a fixed point (`FormRules.update`).
  - SNED/SPED participation and educational-assistance follow-ups.
- **Employment section (member-scoped)**:
  - Seven-day work/pay/profit question, primary occupation, industry, employment type, weekly hours with strict numeric bounds 0–168, job-seeking status, and availability shown only when `worked = No` and `looking = Yes`.
- **Member isolation**: education and employment answers key to `(interviewId, memberId, section, field)`; switching members never leaks drafts; verified against a real Room database file closed and reopened on device.
- **Tests**: new/updated `EducationEmploymentBranchingTest`, `EducationEmploymentMemberIsolationTest`, `EducationEmploymentValidationTest`, `EmploymentValidationTest` (JVM) and `EducationEmploymentPersistenceTest` (device).
