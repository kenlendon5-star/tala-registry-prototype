# D04 — Reusable Compose form controls

Implemented and verified on 5 October 2026. Day 4's representative-section acceptance is complete on OPPO CPH2529, Android 15 / API 35.

## Delivered

- Kotlin field/section models for text, textarea, phone, integer, date, local date/time, choice, select and multi-select, with optional fields, limits and declarative conditions.
- Pure `FormRules`: strict calendar/appointment checks, numeric bounds, phone format, option membership, exclusive None/Prefer not to answer, and cascading clearing of hidden answers independent of field order.
- Reusable `SectionForm` with labeled controls, calendar/time pickers, inline errors, and scroll/focus to the first invalid field on Check.
- `FormSessionViewModel`: immutable drafts keyed by typed household/member identities, with separate validation state per scope and section.
- **Try a practice interview** in household overview and section preview. **Answering for** switches between the household and members without changing the identity of earlier answers.

The practice section intentionally combines fields from `forms.js` to exercise all input types. It is not an official questionnaire section or a claim that all 18 native sections are complete. Photos/signatures remain later work. Answers are session-only; validation never claims a durable save.
